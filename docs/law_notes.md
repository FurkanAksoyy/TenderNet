# Law notes: Art. 21(b) of Law 4734 and KHK 694 (checked 2026-09-26)

Each item was checked against the primary text (Resmî Gazete or mevzuat.gov.tr) unless it is marked otherwise.

## 1. Law No. 7144 (2018): amendment to Art. 21(b)

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Law | 7144, *Bazı Kanunlarda Değişiklik Yapılması Hakkında Kanun* (omnibus law) | RG index 25.05.2018 |
| Adopted (Kabul Tarihi) | **16 May 2018** | RG text |
| Published | **Resmî Gazete, 25 May 2018, No. 30431** | RG text header: "25 Mayıs 2018 CUMA, Resmî Gazete Sayı: 30431" |
| Relevant article | **Art. 11** of Law 7144 | RG text |
| Entry into force | On publication, **25 May 2018** (Art. 22: "Bu Kanun yayımı tarihinde yürürlüğe girer") | RG text |

**What changed.** Art. 11 inserted a new phrase into Art. 21(1)(b) of Law 4734, directly after the words "beklenmeyen veya". The inserted wording is:

> "yapım tekniği açısından özellik arz eden veya yapı veya can ve mal güvenliğinin sağlanması açısından ivedilikle yapılması gerekliliği idarece belirlenen hallerde veyahut"

- **Before, as worded since Law 5812 of 2008:** *"Doğal afetler, salgın hastalıklar, can veya mal kaybı tehlikesi gibi ani ve beklenmeyen veya idare tarafından önceden öngörülemeyen olayların ortaya çıkması üzerine ihalenin ivedi olarak yapılmasının zorunlu olması."*
- **After, from 25 May 2018:** *"Doğal afetler, salgın hastalıklar, can veya mal kaybı tehlikesi gibi ani ve beklenmeyen veya yapım tekniği açısından özellik arz eden veya yapı veya can ve mal güvenliğinin sağlanması açısından ivedilikle yapılması gerekliliği idarece belirlenen hallerde veyahut idare tarafından önceden öngörülemeyen olayların ortaya çıkması üzerine ihalenin ivedi olarak yapılmasının zorunlu olması."*

**What this means.** The amendment widened 21(b) from emergencies that are sudden or unforeseeable to two further cases:
- cases the **contracting authority itself declares** urgent for building, life or property safety;
- works with special construction-technique features.

The new grounds are aimed at *yapım* (works). They add discretion, because it is now the authority that "determines" (idarece belirlenen) that urgency exists. For TenderNet, 2018 is therefore a candidate structural break in the use of 21(b). Our sample is IT goods and services, so the direct effect should be small, and any break we find would more likely reflect general practice or other factors than the new text. This is an inference, not a verified finding.

## 2. Other amendments to 21(b), 2010–2026

- According to Lexpera's consolidated history of Art. 21, **the text of 21(b) changed only twice**:
  - Law **5812** of 20.11.2008 (RG 05.12.2008 / 27075). This set the wording that applied from 2010 to May 2018.
  - Law **7144** of 16.05.2018 (RG 25.05.2018 / 30431).
- **No later amendment to 21(b) was found up to the fetch date** (2026-09-26). Lexpera flags that its display alone cannot prove completeness, so this is marked **"no amendment found"**, not "verified none".
- Not verified, and not amendments to 21(b):
  - The annual KİK threshold updates under Art. 67, which change the monetary limit in **21(f)**.
  - Emergency-period practice, such as heavy 21(b) use during COVID-19 in 2020 and after the February 2023 earthquakes. These changed usage, not the statute. Any claim about them needs its own source.

## 3. KHK 694 (2017): abolition of Türkiye Kamu Hastaneleri Kurumu (TKHK)

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument | KHK No. **694**, *Olağanüstü Hal Kapsamında Bazı Düzenlemeler Yapılması Hakkında KHK* | mevzuat.gov.tr 4.5.694 |
| Decided by the Council of Ministers | **15 August 2017** | mevzuat.gov.tr |
| Published | **Resmî Gazete, 25 August 2017, No. 30165** | mevzuat.gov.tr header and RG index 25.08.2017 |
| Entry into force | On publication, **25 August 2017** (Art. 204) | mevzuat.gov.tr |
| Key article | **Art. 203** | mevzuat.gov.tr |

**Content (Art. 203)**
- **(1)(ğ)** removes "53) Türkiye Halk Sağlığı Kurumu" and "54) Türkiye Kamu Hastaneleri Kurumu" from schedule (I) of Law 5018. This deletes them as separate budget and contracting entities.
- **(1)(ı)** repeals Arts. 26 and 29–34 of KHK 663 (2011), the articles that set up these institutes.
- **(2)** states: *"Diğer mevzuatta Türkiye Kamu Hastaneleri Kurumu ve Türkiye Halk Sağlığı Kurumuna yapılmış olan atıflar Sağlık Bakanlığına yapılmış sayılır."* In other words, references to TKHK in other legislation are to be read as references to the Ministry of Health.
- Secondary sources add that the functions became general directorates of the Ministry of Health, including the Kamu Hastaneleri Genel Müdürlüğü, and that the Kamu Hastane Birlikleri were dissolved. This detail comes from a sector union and TDB news pages, not from the article text read here.

**Warning:** some websites give the RG number as **30168**, for example diyarbakirism.saglik.gov.tr. Both the official RG daily index and mevzuat.gov.tr give **30165**. Use 30165.

**What this means for TenderNet.** From 25 August 2017, hospital contracting authorities that sat under TKHK (the Kamu Hastane Birlikleri general secretariats) were re-labelled as or merged into Ministry of Health provincial units (İl Sağlık Müdürlükleri). This fits the "KHB 2017 kırılması" already in the analysis: a break in *institution identity* is expected around 2017-08/09. Before any temporal comparison, entities should be harmonised across this date.
